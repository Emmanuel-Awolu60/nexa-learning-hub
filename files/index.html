Started with one pot and a stubborn grandmother.

Ruby Leaf began in a family kitchen in Surulere. The recipe was simple: dried hibiscus, ginger, cloves, and patience. Most shop zobo is rushed and sweet. Ours is slow and tart, with sugar added last and only a little.

Today we brew in small batches of 40 litres, so every bottle is fresh. No powders, no colouring, no preservatives. If it tastes like the zobo you grew up with, we did our job.

From dried leaf to cold bottle

  1. Wash and soak

    We rinse the hibiscus, then soak it in hot water with ginger and cloves.

  2. Steep for 48 hours

    Slow steeping pulls out colour and flavour without the bitterness that comes from boiling too long.

  3. Sweeten, chill, bottle

    Fruit and a little sugar go in last. Then it's strained, chilled, and sealed in glass.
